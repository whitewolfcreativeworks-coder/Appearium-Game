# Audio credits

All nature recordings in `assets/audio/` were sourced from Wikimedia Commons and OpenGameArt.org under
public-domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA licences. Files were trimmed, filtered (high-pass), loudness/peak
normalised, looped (beds: ~2 s crossfade) and re-encoded to MP3 for the game; these are modifications of the
originals. Files derived from CC BY-SA sources are themselves distributed under the same CC BY-SA licence.
`beds/campfire_1.mp3` is a randomised granular resequence of the two short OpenGameArt fire recordings credited.
